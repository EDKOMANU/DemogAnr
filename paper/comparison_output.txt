Note: the two life expectancy values differ (79.08 vs 78.06) because the packages use
different assumptions for the open age interval and for nAx in the closed intervals
(DemoTools's "un" method refines nAx iteratively and extrapolates the open interval with a
parametric mortality law; DemogAnr's lifetable_nqx() uses the simpler nax = n/2
approximation, a limitation documented in its own help page). Neither matches Preston's
published e0 = 78.92 exactly, because that value depends on the master life table's own
separation factors, which DemogAnr's lifetable() reproduces exactly when passed via the
nax argument (see the package vignette). The comparison here is about the shape and
labelling of the returned object, not about which package's default assumptions are more
accurate.
